## Chapter 01 Introduction 

### 1.1 Introduction 
La Vogue Vista is a highly advanced, cross-platform virtual beauty try-on mobile application powered by Artificial Intelligence (AI). The platform seamlessly synthesizes augmented reality (AR), computer vision, and on-device machine learning to simulate a comprehensive catalog of beauty transformations directly on the user's smartphone. The application currently supports photorealistic facial cosmetics (lipsticks, eyeshadow, eyeliner, mascara) and is actively expanding to encompass full-body grooming mechanics, specifically **Live Hair Color/Style Modifications** and **Nail Polish Try-On Modes**.

Beyond simple AR overlays, the application introduces a sophisticated "AI Beauty Assistant." This engine actively analyzes the user's physical traits—calculating their distinct Face Shape (e.g., Oval, Square) via biometric ratios and estimating skin undertones—to generate intelligent, mathematically-backed recommendations for haircut styles and cosmetic shades that inherently flatter their natural geometry.

### 1.2 Problem Definition 
The local digital beauty sector suffers from extreme consumer friction. When purchasing aesthetic products—ranging from expensive lipsticks to permanent hair dyes or specialized nail polishes—consumers must blindly trust 2D color swatches displayed on a screen. Because lighting, screen calibration, and individual skin tones drastically alter the final physical appearance of these products, consumers experience excessive shopping cart abandonment and high rates of post-purchase regret.

Current market AR solutions are insufficient: they offer cartoonish filters rather than photorealistic physically-based rendering (PBR), they habitually fail to evaluate darker skin tones accurately, and they treat disparate beauty routines (makeup, nails, hair) as entirely disconnected silos. Furthermore, almost none of these commercial applications explain *why* a specific style works, leaving the user guessing.

### 1.3 Project Objectives 
*   **Comprehensive Face & Hair AR:** Facilitate real-time, photorealistic applications of cosmetics and dynamic hair-color/hairstyle transformations at 30-60 FPS using on-device ML segmentation.
*   **Nail Try-On Mode:** Implement a robust hand-tracking system to successfully isolate fingernails and project interactive polish finishes (matte, glossy, metallic) onto the user's hands in real time.
*   **AI Face Shape & Undertone Assistant:** Develop a recommender system that analyzes facial geometries, determines the objective face shape, and outputs an explainable, 2-line rationale ("Why this shade/style?") for every product recommendation.
*   **Privacy-First Edge Processing:** Guarantee absolute privacy by executing all facial, hand, and hair tracking pipelines locally (via MediaPipe/TFLite tensors) without ever uploading camera feeds to external clouds.

## Chapter 03 Requirements Specification 

### 3.1 Functional Requirements 
*   **AR Cosmetics & E-Commerce:** The system shall track facial landmarks to map lip, eye, and full-face foundation cosmetics accurately, linked directly to a Firebase-driven product catalog.
*   **Semantic Hair Tracking:** The system shall dynamically isolate hair pixels from the surrounding background video feed to apply multiplicative color-shading overlays in real time.
*   **Nail segmentation and Rendering:** The system shall engage hand-tracking logic to precisely mask fingernail boundaries, simulating physically-based rendering texturing (e.g., specular gloss vs. diffuse matte finishes).
*   **AI Beauty Assistant & Face Shape Matcher:** The application shall utilize a selfie to calculate the biometric span of the user's jawline to categorize their Face Shape, fetching and recommending appropriate volumetric hair styles from the database alongside a 2-line visual rationale.

### 3.2 Non-Functional Requirements 
*   **Real-Time Responsiveness (FPS):** Concurrent tracking and rendering overlays (whether Face, Hair, or Hands) must maintain a baseline of 30 frames per second to prevent motion sickness and maintain the mirror illusion.
*   **Cross-Mode Fidelity:** The application must ensure color rendering consistency (ΔE ≤ 3) between the Live AR Camera view and static uploaded photo modes.
*   **Absolute Privacy:** No biometric telemetry, camera session data, or mesh vectors may be transmitted off the device. 

### 3.3 Hardware / Software Requirements 
*   **Hardware Devices:** Modern Android or iOS smartphone; NPUs (Neural Processing Units) heavily recommended for concurrent inference loads.
*   **Software OS:** iOS 14.0+ ; Android 10.0+
*   **Development Stack:**
    *   **Frontend UI:** Flutter Framework (Dart)
    *   **Native Rendering Bridge:** Swift/Metal (iOS) and Kotlin/OpenGL (Android)
    *   **Computer Vision:** Google MediaPipe (Face & Hand Landmarkers) + TensorFlow Lite (Hair Segmentation)
    *   **Backend:** Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Functions)

## Chapter 04 Feasibility Study 

### 4.1 Operational Feasibility 
La Vogue Vista addresses a fundamental psychological barrier in e-commerce: risk aversion. By providing highly realistic AR try-ons across the three major beauty pillars (Face, Hair, Nails), the application consolidates the entire vanity ecosystem into a single, intuitive interface. Standardized UI controls (horizontal scrolling catalogs, simple color-picker swatches) minimize the learning curve, ensuring rapid adoption across varying age demographics and technological literacy levels.

### 4.2 Economical Feasibility
Developing separate native apps for complex computer vision is traditionally cost-prohibitive. Leveraging the Flutter framework circumvents this by providing a unified UI codebase. Furthermore, integrating a serverless architecture (Firebase) eliminates the need to pay for dedicated routing servers; the project runs entirely within Google's free-tier usage limits. Finally, because all heavy ML tracking runs *on the user's phone*, there are zero cloud-compute rendering bills, making scaling financially trivial.

### 4.3 Technical Feasibility 
Running three vastly different machine learning paradigms (Dense Face Mesh, 2D Semantic Hair Segmentation, and 3D Skeletal Hand Tracking) simultaneously on a mobile device introduces high memory and thermal risks. However, the system architecture purposefully silos these tracking engines. Users select distinct modes (e.g., entering the "Nail Salon" vs "Hair Studio"), enabling the `NativeVisionPlugin` to efficiently load and unload specific `.tflite` models sequentially from the GPU, preserving RAM and guaranteeing 30+ FPS tracking.

## Chapter 06 Development Tools and Technologies 

### 6.1 Development Methodology 
The application was built utilizing an **Agile Software Development Methodology (Scrum)**. Given the intrinsic volatility of augmented reality physics—where a decimal error in a native matrix translates to eyelashes floating inches off a face—rigid Waterfall planning is obsolete. Agile permitted the team to execute immediate compilation routines, enabling rapid visual testing, immediate matrix adjustments, and concurrent feature branching (e.g., simultaneously building the Dart UI while the Swift AR engine was isolated under a separate branch).

### 6.2 Programming Languages and Tools 
*   **Dart (Flutter SDK):** Responsible for the cross-platform generation of the e-commerce views, maintaining highly fluid 60Hz scroll performance across dense catalogs.
*   **Swift and Kotlin Native:** Explicitly relied upon for traversing C++ MediaPipe wrappers, hijacking Apple's `AVFoundation` (or Android's `Camera2`), and forcing GPU hardware scaling overrides.
*   **TensorFlow Lite:** Crucial environment for executing quantized (`INT8` or `Float16`) segmentation matrices on lower-end mobile cores efficiently.

### 6.3 Third-Party Components and Libraries 
*   **MediaPipe Vision Suite:** The ultimate on-device CV backbone. The `FaceMesh` task guarantees 468-point realism for cosmetics, while the `HandLandmarker` ensures hyper-accurate localized tracking specifically required for rapid finger movements in the Nail Try-On.
*   **Firebase / Stripe Test:** The entire data spine is managed by Firebase NoSQL ecosystems, and user checkout workflows will be validated against the Stripe API Sandbox environment to prove E-Commerce viability.

### 6.4 Algorithms 
The true brilliance of La Vogue Vista resides in the complex Vision and Rendering algorithms:
*   **Physical Based Rendering (PBR) Proxies:** To simulate matte versus glossy nails or sheer glosses over lips, the native drawing algorithms manipulate `Specular Highlights` mathematically. A glossy nail finish algorithm artificially injects a bright, sharp, white polygon curve over the base color hex to mimic intense light reflection. A matte finish executes a diffuse, heavy blur to eliminate specular bouncing.
*   **Biometric Shape Classifier:** The AI Beauty algorithm calculates Euclidean distances between MediaPipe vectors (e.g., Jaw_Left to Jaw_Right vs. Forehead_Top to Chin_Bottom). By parsing these geometric ratios through a lightweight decision tree (`if width ≈ height -> Round`), it independently classifies the face shape.

### 7.4 Challenges Encountered and Solutions
*   **Challenge: UI Thread Starvation & Dropped Frames:** Early prototypes routed heavy CV matrices from the camera directly through Dart memory space, instantly crashing the UI event loop and crushing performance below 10 FPS.
    *   *Solution:* Architecturally detached the entire computer-vision loop into the native Swift/Metal boundaries. Flutter merely passes parameter strings ("Set Color #FF0000"), allowing the native OS to handle the immense hardware processing asynchronously, resolving latency entirely.
*   **Challenge: Spatial Memory Heap Leaks:** The 30 FPS render cycle dynamically generated complex graphical sublayers that compounded infinitely without garbage collection, triggering catastrophic Out-Of-Memory application crashes in under 5 seconds.
    *   *Solution:* Engineered strict recursive layer sweeps within the native Swift drawing routine that force the explicit deletion of all previously drawn geometric shapes from GPU RAM before executing the subsequent millimeter calculation.

### 7.5 Current System Limitations
*   *Android Parity:* The AR engine heavily relies on Apple-specific frameworks (AVFoundation/Metal). The Android counterpart requires an extensive architectural parallel rewrite mapping Kotlin against the CameraX API sequence.
*   *ML Memory Footprints:* Loading multiple MediaPipe `.task` bundles and TFLite segmentation graphs into heap memory poses significant RAM initialization challenges requiring complex asynchronous loading sequences to prevent UI hanging.
